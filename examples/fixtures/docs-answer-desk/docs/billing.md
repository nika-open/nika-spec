# Billing and plans

Tidewren bills per workspace, monthly or yearly.

## Plans

The Free plan covers three calendars and five members. The Team plan removes both limits and adds shared availability views.

## Change your plan

Workspace owners change the plan under Settings, then Billing. An upgrade starts at once; a downgrade starts at the next billing date.

## Refunds

Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded, and switching plans never triggers a refund.
