# Sharing calendars

Share a calendar with teammates and control what each of them can change.

## Invite a teammate

Open the calendar, choose Share, and type the teammate's work email. They receive an invitation link that stays valid for seven days.

## Permission levels

Viewers see events. Editors add, move and delete events. Owners also manage sharing and can transfer ownership to another editor.

## Revoke access

Open Share, find the teammate in the member list and choose Remove. Their access ends at once, and events they created stay on the calendar.
