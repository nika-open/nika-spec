# Your data

You own your calendars. Export them at any time, or delete your account.

## Export your data

Owners open Settings, then Data, and choose Export. The archive is emailed to the owner within one hour and the download link lasts 48 hours.

## Export formats

Calendars export as iCalendar files (.ics). Member lists and event history export as CSV.

## Delete your account

Deleting an account removes its calendars after a 30-day grace period. Shared calendars move to their next owner instead of being removed.
